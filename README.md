# Microsoft Purview Healthcare Playbook

## Overview
This repository contains the **9-Phase Microsoft Purview & Defender Implementation Playbook** authored by Ghunchas Healthcare AI. 

This blueprint bridges the gap between strict HIPAA/HITRUST compliance requirements and the rapid adoption of Generative AI. It contains live lab screenshots and technical configurations to automate data discovery, enforce Zero-Trust access, and generate defensible audit evidence.

## The 9 Phases Covered
1. **Data Map & Unified Catalog:** Mapping Azure, Fabric, and Odoo ERP.
2. **Information Protection:** Auto-labeling and Exact Data Match (EDM) classifiers.
3. **DLP:** Blocking email, endpoint (USB/Clipboard), and Copilot data leaks.
4. **Insider Risk Management:** Detecting exfiltration while maintaining privacy (pseudonymization).
5. **Compliance Manager:** Mapping controls to HITRUST and tracking the compliance score.
6. **Communication Compliance:** Scanning Teams and emails for inappropriate text.
7. **DSPM for AI:** Securing Microsoft 365 Copilot and third-party AI apps (ChatGPT).
8. **Data Lifecycle:** Retention policies, 10-year immutable records, and audit logs.
9. **eDiscovery:** Legal holds, KQL searches, and Chain of Custody exports.
